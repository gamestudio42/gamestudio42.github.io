03 / THE WAY WE BUILD

Made to play.
Open to possibility.

We build worlds where decisions leave a mark: a canal brings a district to life, a supply route keeps a squad standing, a block becomes a shelter. At Game Studio 42, simulation is the starting point for play. Every game opens in your browser, and every system is there to be understood and changed.

Built together, in the open.

Game Studio 42 brings together independent creation and open collaboration. Behind each game are people writing code, drawing worlds and caring for the details. Play, share an idea or help shape the next version.

04 / GROW WITH THE COMMUNITY

Your project can grow with Game Studio 42.

Bring your open-source game or game-development technology: engines, frameworks, libraries, SDKs, tools and AI agents for games. Any engine. Any platform.

01 /

Shared maintenance

Stay involved as a maintainer and develop your project alongside other contributors.

02 /

Community adoption

Propose a new chapter for your project and find maintainers to carry it forward.

Repository hosting and shared collaboration after approval. Catalog promotion is evaluated separately.

Propose a projectHow it worksOpen source

Clear defaults. Room for each project.

We prioritize AGPL-3.0-or-later for games and multiplayer servers, and Apache-2.0 for reusable technology. MIT, BSD, GPL and other open-source licenses are welcome for evaluation. Exceptions are documented; joining does not require automatic relicensing. Existing projects keep their licenses.

Read the licensing guide

Public proposals on GitHub · English or Portuguese

Projects still in development are welcome when there is code to review. Acceptance requires review and a confirmed maintainer; adoption proposals can wait for volunteers. Authorship, credits and history are preserved.
